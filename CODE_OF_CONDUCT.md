# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and
respect differing experiences and viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and publication of
private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to <mcc+civiverify@polbeo.de>. Maintainers will
review reports fairly and may take appropriate action, including warnings or
removal from project spaces.
